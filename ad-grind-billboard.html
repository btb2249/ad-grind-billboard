Straight answers

Is this real advertising, or just inside the app?

Both, and here's the honest split. Boosts put your thing in front of every Ad Grind player — the in-app billboard, the ad feed, and pop-ins between games. This page is the part that lives on the open web: anyone with the link can see the top spots, and it posts cleanly to Facebook. What credits can't do is buy space on Google or Facebook's own ad networks — those take cash through an ad account, and no game currency changes that.

Can I just buy credits with money?

No. Credits are earned by playing and doing tasks, full stop. That's the whole point of the board: position comes from grinding, not from whoever has the biggest wallet.

How long does my spot stay up?

As long as your boost clock runs. Rotation spots are bought by the hour (or locked for a full day), top-3 and #1 takeovers by the hour. When the clock runs out, your spot stays in the general Ad Grind rotation but comes off this public board until you boost again.

Someone out-boosted me. What happens?

They take the higher spot and you slide down one — your remaining boost time still counts at the tier below. That's the game working as intended. Grind more, boost harder, take it back.

What can I put on the board?

Anything that's yours to promote and legal to advertise: a business, an app, a website, a service, an item for sale. If it has a name and a link, it can go up. Spots that point at scams or other people's stuff get pulled.

Who runs Ad Grind?

Ad Grind is an independent app project out of San Angelo, Texas. This billboard is its public face — the in-app board and this page run off the same boosts.
